# 🏥 VitalStep - Precision Hand Health Analytics

**VitalStep** is a premium, flagship-grade medical application designed for precision grip strength analysis and hand health tracking. Built with a focus on "Apple/Meta tier" aesthetics and intuitive data visualization, VitalStep bridges the gap between clinical assessment and patient-friendly analytics.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎨 Premium Medical UI/UX
- **Elegant Design System**: Features a high-contrast medical teal palette with soft "glassmorphic" surfaces and fluid micro-animations.
- **Adaptive Dashboards**: Intelligent home screens that provide instant AI-driven summaries of your hand health and symmetry.

### 📊 Advanced Visualization & Trends
- **Interactive Multi-Mode Charts**: Toggle between **Line Charts** (for trend tracking) and **Bar Charts** (for side-by-side hand comparison).
- **Flexible Periodicity**: View your recovery progress over **Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly** intervals with a single tap.
- **Health Insight AI**: Automated analysis of grip tests to identify asymmetry and progress metrics.

### 📲 Medical Communication & Reporting
- **Professional PDF Reports**: Generate comprehensive clinical history reports with built-in previews and native sharing.
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Distributed your reports or "Share Chart" screenshots to specialists instantly via one-tap WhatsApp integration.
- **Specialist Hub**: Directly call or message healthcare providers from within the app.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Framework**: [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/) - Multi-platform efficiency with premium performance.
- **Architecture**: [Stacked](https://stacked.filledstacks.com/) - Clean, testable, and maintainable software architecture.
- **Backend**: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) - Real-time medical data storage and security.
- **Visualization**: [FL Chart](https://pub.dev/packages/fl_chart) - High-performance, interactive data rendering.
- **Reporting**: [Printing](https://pub.dev/packages/printing) & [PDF](https://pub.dev/packages/pdf) - Clinical-grade document generation.
